Procedure

Applying the Fertilizer


Step

Description

Image

1

Bake 48 Eggshells at 79.5°C for 10 minutes

2

Crush Eggshells by Hand in Plastic Bag

3

Grind Eggshells into a Fine Powder with Food Processor

4

Mix 180 mL Powder into 3.6 L Room-Temperature Water

5

Boil 200 mL Dry Lentils in 2 L Water

6

Repeat Process Until 3.6 L of Water can be Filtered Out

7

Supplement Fertilizers for Each Plant's Weekly Watering According to their Ratio
